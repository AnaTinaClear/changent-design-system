Semantic / status palette

Not brand colors — chosen for contrast and colorblind safety. Each status has a fill, a text variant (passes 4.5:1 on white), and an on-fill text color.

Two traps: the amber #F3C11B and cyan #3FC4E0 fills need dark text on top — white is invisible on amber (1.69:1). Always reinforce a status with an icon + label.
